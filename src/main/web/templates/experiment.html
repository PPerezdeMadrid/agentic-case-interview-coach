Per-dimension: deviation from reference & hit rate

For every repeat where a graph returned a real number for this dimension (not not_tested) and the scenario has an author-defined expected score, this compares the two. Mean deviation is the signed average of (judge score − expected score): + means the graph overestimates on average, − means it underestimates, close to 0 means it lands on target. % exact match is the share of those comparisons where the judge's score matched the expected score exactly — treat it as anecdotal when the compared-count in parentheses is small.
