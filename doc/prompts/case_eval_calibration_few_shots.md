## case_opening

Score 1: Interviewer gives the case prompt. Candidate: "Okay, so, losses are widening despite 60% revenue growth… right. Let's look at the cost structure then. I think the first step is to break down the unit economics: what does it cost to acquire a customer, and what's the ongoing cost to service them?"
Score: 1 — there is no recap of the situation and no clarifying question about the objective anywhere in this turn; the candidate goes straight into hypothesis-driven analysis. A confident, fluent tone is not a recap. Do not credit this turn with "a solid recap" or "asking about the objective" unless you can point to the literal sentence that does it — a rationale that can't quote it is not grounded in the transcript.
